Room One · The Hallway

You already know something is off. Here's what it's called.

Three patterns Dr. Sippio has diagnosed across public, government, education, and corporate organizations — the ones most leaders learn to live with instead of confronting.

"Everyone's nodding. No one agrees."

Cognitive Conflict — the fracture between what your people genuinely think and what they perform in the room. It shows up in who stops speaking first.

"The org chart still works. The people don't."

Hollow Shell Organizations — operations that keep running on the outside while exhaustion and disengagement hollow it out within.

"Something in the room won't let people say the real thing."

The Human Interference Field — the invisible layer of politics and static that distorts every message before it lands.

Left alone, these don't stay flat — they compound. Every interaction either deposits into or withdraws from the trust your organization runs on.
